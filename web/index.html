Help

Space
Straight key: the tone sounds while it is held
← →
Paddles: dit and dah (swap them in Settings). Iambic A, Iambic B, Ultimatic, Bug and Straight key modes
Touch
The Dit, Key and Dah buttons key like the keyboard
USB pad
A paddle seen as mouse buttons: set Key with to USB pad, keep the pointer over the page. Left is dit, right dah
+ −
Receiver sensitivity: higher catches weaker signals, lower rejects noise
< >
Tune by 10 Hz, or click the waterfall
z
Zero-beat: tune to the strongest signal
b B a s
Narrower, wider filter; AFC; lock the receive speed
TX/RX
Listens by default. Touch the key to transmit: the receiver goes quiet and comes back after the break-in delay
Training
Enter starts, checks and moves on. A wrong character restarts the item. Tab replays (copy) or toggles the hint (send), Esc goes back to the setup
