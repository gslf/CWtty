    Help

    Space
    Straight key: the tone sounds while it is held
    ← →
    Paddles: dit and dah (swap them in Settings). Iambic A, Iambic B, Ultimatic, Bug and Straight key modes
    Touch
    The Dit, Key and Dah buttons key like the keyboard
    USB pad
    A paddle seen as mouse buttons: set Key with to USB pad, keep the pointer over the page. Left is dit, right dah
    USB key
    A straight key seen as a mouse button: set Key with to USB straight key, keep the pointer over the page
    + −
    Receiver sensitivity: higher catches weaker signals, lower rejects noise
    < >
    Tune by 10 Hz, or tap the waterfall
    z
    Zero-beat: tune to the strongest signal
    b B a s
    Narrower, wider filter; AFC; lock the receive speed
    TX/RX
    Listens by default. Touch the key to transmit: the receiver goes quiet and comes back after the break-in delay
    Training
    Choose Receive or Send, then Start. Enter checks and moves on; a wrong character restarts the item. Tab replays (copy) or toggles the hint (send). Setup (or Esc) changes the session, and Resume brings it back while nothing changed
    Level
    Copy: how many times an item can be heard, 4 at level 1 down to 1 at level 4. Out of listens, a wrong answer shows the item
    Records
    The best streak of correct answers, one per copy level and one for send. Beat it for a NEW RECORD
    Stats
    Records, accuracy, time, listens, sending speed, the last four weeks and each character. Kept in this browser; Export saves them to a file, Import brings a file back (it replaces them, after asking), Cloud keeps every device in sync on Filen, end-to-end encrypted (two-factor sign-in too): the totals are the sum of the devices
    Rooms
    Online rooms: everybody in a room hears everybody, each station on its own pitch (around Remote pitch in Settings), from this app or the terminal one. A password makes the room private and encrypted. Leaving the tab leaves the room
    K
    In a room, K passes the turn and ends your line, as on the air; so do KN, BK, AR and SK, and BT (=) starts a new paragraph. A line also ends when another station starts transmitting, or after a pause
